# README hero image — final-pass prompt

Using the supplied cockpit image as a composition reference, create a refined, entirely original README hero image that looks convincingly like a technically ambitious 1988 Amiga flight simulator.

Preserve these successful features of the reference:

- The wide, low cockpit occupying approximately the lower 40% of the frame.
- The small central HUD combiner.
- The broad, mostly empty sky and flat ocean horizon.
- The distant, minimal carrier and geometric coastline silhouettes.
- The dark instrument panel, two green displays, central attitude indicator, small gauges and restrained warning lights.
- The calm forward-flight composition rather than a dramatic action scene.

## Required rendering process

Design the image at exactly **320×200 logical pixels**. Render every edge, symbol and instrument directly on that pixel grid. Then enlarge it to exactly **1280×800** using **4× nearest-neighbor scaling only**.

The final enlarged image must retain square 4×4 pixel blocks. Do not render at high resolution and apply a pixel-art filter afterward.

## Exterior view

- Replace the smooth sky gradient with only **five or six flat horizontal blue-violet color bands**.
- Each sky band must have a hard pixel boundary. No smooth blending, airbrushing, glow or anti-aliasing.
- Use a nearly straight horizon with a dark navy sea below it.
- The sea may contain a few short, one-pixel horizontal highlights in two muted blue shades.
- Keep the coastline extremely distant and geometric: a handful of stepped polygon silhouettes using no more than four colors.
- Keep the carrier tiny and near the horizon. Represent it with a simple flat silhouette and only a few pixels of superstructure.
- Leave most of the sky empty. Do not add clouds, a bridge, a city skyline, nearby aircraft, missiles, explosions or afterburners.

## HUD and cockpit

- Keep the HUD combiner small, central and transparent, with a thin dark-grey one-pixel frame.
- Use sparse pale-green HUD graphics: a tiny central cross, short horizon marks, minimal heading ticks, and only a few tiny block-font numerals.
- Do not enlarge the HUD or fill it with modern symbology.
- Keep the cockpit nearly black, with angular charcoal panels and thin medium-grey one-pixel outlines.
- Retain two compact green monochrome displays, but simplify their contents into sparse abstract flight and navigation marks.
- Retain a small central red-and-blue attitude sphere and two tiny round gauges.
- Indicator lights should be small rectangles using restrained green, red, amber and dull blue.
- Labels must use a tiny, low-contrast bitmap font. Prefer short generic abbreviations. Avoid crisp modern typography and large readable words.
- Make the left and right panel geometry slightly asymmetrical and utilitarian, as though constrained by memory and screen space.

## Palette and pixel restrictions

- Use no more than **24 colors in the entire image**.
- Favor black, charcoal, cool grey, muted blue-violet, dark navy, subdued green, dull red and amber.
- Use large flat color regions.
- Every visible line must be at least one logical pixel wide and aligned to the 320×200 grid.
- Use sparse ordered dithering only where one flat color meets another; do not dither the whole image.
- No alpha transparency, subpixel detail, anti-aliasing, smooth gradients, texture painting, bloom, reflections, lens effects, soft shadows or realistic lighting.
- No CRT scanlines, screen curvature, phosphor glow or artificial monitor frame.
- Do not imitate polished contemporary pixel art. It should look functional, sparse, slightly crude and genuinely limited by 1988 hardware.

## Originality requirements

Create new cockpit geometry, display contents, HUD symbols, coastline, carrier silhouette and palette. Do not reproduce any specific screenshot, bitmap, cockpit panel, map, aircraft model, title screen, logo, text or other asset from *F/A-18 Interceptor*.

Do not include the game title, Electronic Arts branding, trademarks, signatures or a watermark.

## Negative prompt

Smooth sky gradient, airbrushed sky, photorealistic lighting, modern flight simulator, detailed city, Golden Gate Bridge, dramatic clouds, nearby carrier, formation aircraft, missiles, explosions, afterburner flames, glossy cockpit, reflections, large canopy frame, oversized HUD, modern glass cockpit, high-resolution gauges, crisp typography, vector graphics, anti-aliasing, bloom, lens flare, depth of field, CRT filter, scanlines, modern pixel art, VGA graphics, 256-color artwork, more than 24 colors, post-processed pixelation.
