# Note 08 section 7 (second version: stepuntil on the step counter V:4A992 = N; the key pointer then reads N+1, i.e. the reader of step N has run; the player record is read at that blank and again 3 blanks later, rec2: equal means the step's motion had already been done at the first sample): the demo played from demo.vasnap (step 47 of the stream) to its end, with a checkpoint of the player record every
# 100 stream steps: the key pointer V:54E5C = start + k (start = 0xC544E8 in this snapshot). Per checkpoint: blank counter, step counter,
# game mode, replay mode, crash allowance, view, W0/W2 and the first 0x80 bytes of the player record (position +0x14, +0x18, +0x1C,
# attitude +0x66..+0x6A, speed, fuel, commands).  No crash may occur (W0 bit 0x0200, V:4A94F stays 3).
