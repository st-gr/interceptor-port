# From the main menu (training-menu.vasnap) to the security prompt of menu item 3 (training, part 1: mode 2) and of
# item 4 (part 2: mode 0x7D); freezes the challenge in two snapshots. The challenge nibbles are peeked
# (F:1D02A, four words) so the answer can be looked up in disks/countercodes.txt.
# Writes extracted/captures/training-code-3.vasnap and training-code-4.vasnap and the two prompt frames. The challenges of the committed
# scripts (194A for item 3, 3CCE for item 4) are those of the snapshots made on 2026-10-14; make the snapshots again and the challenge changes.
restore extracted/captures/training-menu.vasnap
seglist extracted/interceptor.bin extracted/interceptor.map.json
key 3
freeze
stepuntil F:4A802 2 eq 4 3000
step 150
peek F:1D02A 8 chal3
peek F:4A95E 1 mode3
frame extracted/captures/training-code-3.ppm
snapshot extracted/captures/training-code-3.vasnap
thaw
wait 4
restore extracted/captures/training-menu.vasnap
seglist extracted/interceptor.bin extracted/interceptor.map.json
key 4
freeze
stepuntil F:4A802 2 eq 4 3000
step 150
peek F:1D02A 8 chal4
peek F:4A95E 1 mode4
frame extracted/captures/training-code-4.ppm
snapshot extracted/captures/training-code-4.vasnap
