# sound-boot: cold boot of the autoboot disk with the audio register trace and the recording; 8,200 frames (164 s of emulated time) are stepped
# (the game loads for about 150 s, plays its start-up sound, shows the credits).
wait 1
freeze
audtrace on frame
wav extracted/captures/audio/sound-boot.wav start
step 8200
wav extracted/captures/audio/sound-boot.wav stop
audtrace off
peek R:BFE001 1 ciaa_pra
peek R:BFE201 1 ciaa_ddra
peek R:BFD000 1 ciab_pra
